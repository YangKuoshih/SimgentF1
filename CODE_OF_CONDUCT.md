# Code of conduct

SimGent follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion
about the project. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted in issues, pull
requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact listed in
`pyproject.toml`. Do not report them in a public issue. Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may edit or remove content, or block
participants, who do not follow this code.
