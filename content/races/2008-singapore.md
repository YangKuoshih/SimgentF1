The inaugural Formula 1 night race at Marina Bay became the center of one of the biggest sporting scandals in history — known as 'Crashgate'.

• **The Setup**: Fernando Alonso started P15 after a fuel pump failure in qualifying. Renault put him on an aggressive ultra-short fuel load, boxing him on Lap 12.
• **The Deliberate Crash**: On Lap 14, teammate Nelson Piquet Jr deliberately crashed his Renault into the barrier at Turn 17, where no crane was available. Under the 2008 regulations, the pit lane was closed when the Safety Car deployed, forcing the leaders to wait or take penalties.
• **Alonso Takes Victory**: Alonso inherited the lead as rivals pitted later, winning the race. The deliberate conspiracy was exposed by Piquet in 2009, leading to lifetime bans (later overturned) for Flavio Briatore and Pat Symonds.
