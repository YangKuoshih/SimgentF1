One year after their 1989 controversy, Ayrton Senna and Alain Prost (now at Ferrari) returned to Suzuka for another championship decider.

• **The Pole Grid Controversy**: Senna qualified on pole but requested the FIA move pole position to the cleaner, grippier racing line on the left. Balestre refused, forcing Senna onto the dirty side of the track.
• **The 250 km/h Collision**: Prost predictably launched better off the line. Into Turn 1, Senna refused to yield, keeping his foot planted and ramming the rear-quarter of Prost's Ferrari at 250 km/h. Both cars careened through the gravel trap into the tyre wall.
• **Instant Title**: With both drivers eliminated 9 seconds into the race, Senna mathematically clinched his 2nd World Championship.
