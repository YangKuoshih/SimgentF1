The 1998 Belgian Grand Prix at Spa remains one of the most dramatic races in history.

• **13-Car Lap 1 Pile-Up**: David Coulthard lost control on the damp descent after La Source, triggering a catastrophic 13-car chain-reaction that littered the circuit with over $10 million in carbon fibre wreckage, forcing a red flag.
• **Schumacher vs Coulthard**: In torrential rain, Michael Schumacher led comfortably by 40 seconds. Approaching Coulthard to lap him on Lap 25, Coulthard eased off on the racing line in blinding spray. Schumacher unsighted rammed into the McLaren's rear, tearing off his right-front wheel.
• **Pit Lane Fury**: Schumacher drove the 3-wheeled Ferrari into the pits and stormed into the McLaren garage shouting 'Are you trying to kill me?!' Damon Hill and Ralf Schumacher went on to score an emotional maiden 1-2 finish for Eddie Jordan's team.
